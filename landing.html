Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

A thoughtfully designed calculator that puts natural language at the center of every interaction.

Natural Language Output

Every result is automatically converted into readable words. "42" becomes "Forty-two" — making math more accessible.

Multi-Language Support

Switch between English and German with a single click. Results adapt instantly to your chosen language.

Text-to-Speech

Hear your results spoken aloud with proper pronunciation in the selected language. Great for accessibility.

Calculation History

Every calculation is saved locally. Review, annotate with remarks, and revisit your work anytime.

Keyboard Support

Type numbers and operators directly from your keyboard. Enter to calculate, Escape to clear — it just works.

Fully Responsive

Beautiful on desktop, tablet, and mobile. The interface adapts seamlessly to any screen size.

How It Works

Simple as 1, 2, 3.

No setup, no downloads, no accounts. Just open and calculate.

01

Enter Your Expression

Click buttons or type on your keyboard. Build any expression using +, −, ×, ÷ and decimal numbers.

02

Get Your Result

Press "=" and instantly see the numeric answer alongside its natural language equivalent.

03

Hear & Explore

Listen to the result spoken aloud, switch languages, or add remarks to your calculation history.

About

Built by students,
for everyone.

VUNA Calculator was created by Group 2 of the 2025/26 Software Engineering class as part of SEN 482. It demonstrates how a simple tool can bridge the gap between numerical output and linguistic comprehension — making mathematics more accessible and human.

The project is open-source and built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and Bootstrap 5. No frameworks, no build steps — just clean, readable code.

Ready to try it?

No installation needed. Open in your browser and start calculating.
